# Roya's Imaginary Cafe — Store Policies

## Hours

All cafés are open 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM Monday through Saturday, and 7:00 AM to
4:00 PM on Sundays. The Cambridge roastery café closes at 2:00 PM on Sundays for
production. Holiday hours are posted at each café and in the app a week ahead;
we're closed on Thanksgiving and on December 25.

## Refunds and Remakes

If you don't like your drink, we will remake it — no questions, no receipt
needed, same visit. Bags of beans can be returned unopened within 14 days with a
receipt. Opened bags can't be returned. Brewing equipment can be returned
within 30 days, unused and in its packaging, with a receipt.

## Payment Methods

We accept all major cards, contactless and mobile wallets, and our own gift
cards and app balance. We've been cashless at the bar since 2023, though the
Cambridge roastery still takes cash for whole-bean retail.

## Dogs

Dogs are welcome on the patios at every location. Inside, only service animals
are permitted, per health code. We keep a jar of dog biscuits at every register —
they are free, and yes, your dog knows about the jar already. Please keep dogs
leashed on the patio and out of other guests' space.

## Wi-Fi and Laptops

Wi-Fi is free and unlimited. Laptops are welcome at all tables on weekdays. On
Saturday and Sunday between 9:00 AM and 1:00 PM, the large community tables are
laptop-free so groups can actually sit together. Outlets are available at the
counter seating; please don't run extension cords across walkways.

## Quiet and Community Spaces

Each café has a quiet corner where we ask guests to take calls and meetings to a
whisper. The large shared tables are first-come, first-served and meant for
sharing — if it's busy, expect to scoot over and make a friend.

## Mobile Orders

Mobile orders through the café app are picked up at the marked shelf. Drinks are
held for 15 minutes; after that we remake iced drinks on request and re-steam
nothing, because re-steamed milk is a crime. You can schedule a mobile order up
to a day ahead, handy for the morning rush.

## Gift Cards

Gift cards never expire and carry no fees. They can be used at any location and
reloaded in-store or in the app. Lost physical cards can be replaced only if the
card was registered in the app. Digital gift cards can be sent by email straight
from the app.

## Loyalty Program

Every dollar spent earns 1 bean. 100 beans = any free drink. Beans expire after
12 months of account inactivity. Free birthday drink during your birthday week,
any size, no bean cost. Loyalty is tracked through the app or a phone number at
the register.

## Accessibility

All six cafés are step-free at the main entrance, with at least one
accessibility-height table and an accessible restroom. Service animals are always
welcome inside. If you need anything to make your visit easier, ask any
teammate — that's what we're here for. Our menus are available in large print and
in the app with a screen-reader-friendly layout.

## Restrooms

Restrooms are for customers and are single-occupancy and all-gender at every
location except Cambridge, which has two multi-stall restrooms. A teammate can
unlock the restroom if it's code-locked during slower hours.

## Lost and Found

Found items are logged and held at the café for 30 days; after that, unclaimed
items are donated. Ask any teammate or message the café in the app to check for
something you've left behind. We can't ship lost items but can hold them for
pickup.

## Photography and Filming

Casual photos for personal use are fine. For professional shoots, commercial
filming, or anything with lighting rigs and crews, email the café first so we can
find a time that doesn't crowd other guests. Please be considerate about
including other people in your shots.

## Weather and Emergency Closures

If we have to close early for weather or an emergency, we post it in the app and
on the café's door as soon as we can. Mobile orders placed for a closed café are
automatically refunded. Safety of our teams and guests comes before staying open.

## Complaints and Feedback

Tell us — to a teammate's face, through the app's feedback form, or by email.
We read everything and reply within two business days. If a visit went wrong, we'd
much rather fix it than have you quietly never come back.
