# Roya's Imaginary Cafe — Employee Handbook

## Welcome

Roya's Imaginary Cafe was founded in 2017 in Cambridge, Massachusetts, with a
single espresso cart outside a downtown marketplace. Today we operate six cafés
across Greater Boston and a roastery in Cambridge.

Our mission: great coffee, zero pretension, and a job you don't dread. This
handbook covers how we work together. If anything here is unclear, ask your café
lead — none of this is meant to be a gotcha.

## Working Hours

Full-time baristas work 36 hours per week across four shifts. Part-time baristas
work fewer than 30 hours and are scheduled in blocks of at least four hours.

Opening shift starts at 5:30 AM. Closing shift ends 30 minutes after the posted
closing time to allow for cleanup, cash-out, and prepping the next morning's
pastry case.

Schedules are posted two weeks in advance in the café app. We try hard to honor
availability you set in the app, but during peak weeks (the first week of
September, and December) everyone is asked to take at least one weekend shift.

## Shift Swaps

Shift swaps are allowed with manager approval at least 24 hours in advance, using
the shift board in the café app. Post the shift you need covered, and a teammate
can claim it; the swap isn't final until your café lead taps "approve." Same-day
swaps need a phone call to your lead, not just an app post — the app isn't
checked minute to minute.

## Breaks

Per Massachusetts law, any shift longer than six hours includes a 30-minute
unpaid meal break. Baristas also get one paid 15-minute rest break per five hours
worked. Breaks are taken away from the bar — sit down, drink water, breathe.

## Time Off

Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid time off per year, plus 6 paid
holidays. PTO accrues from your first day and can be used after 60 days of
employment. Unused PTO rolls over up to a maximum of 5 days per year.

Part-time employees accrue 1 hour of paid sick time for every 30 hours worked, in
line with Massachusetts law. To request time off, submit the request in the café
app at least two weeks ahead for planned days; for sick days, message your lead as
early as you can so we can cover the bar.

## Benefits

Full-time employees become eligible for our health, dental, and vision plans on
the first of the month after 90 days of employment. We cover 70% of the employee
premium. We also offer a monthly transit stipend that covers an MBTA pass, and a
retirement plan with a 3% company match after one year.

Every employee, full- or part-time, gets an Employee Assistance Program line for
free, confidential counseling and financial coaching.

## The Free Drink Policy

Every employee gets one free drink per shift, any size, plus a half-pound bag of
beans each week. Friends-and-family visiting you on shift get 25% off. Please
don't combine this with other discounts — the registers will cry, and so will
accounting.

## Training Ladder

New hires start as a Barista I and train on register, milk steaming, and the
core menu. After a skills check, you move to Barista II (espresso dialing,
pour-over, opening duties) and a pay bump. Barista III leads shifts, trains new
hires, and handles cash-out. Roasters train separately at the Cambridge roastery.
Each level has a written skills checklist in the app; your lead signs off as you
go.

## Dress Code

Closed-toe shoes are required at all times behind the bar. Aprons are provided
and must be worn during service. Beyond that: be clean, be comfortable, be
yourself. Hair longer than shoulder length must be tied back when working with
the roaster or espresso machines. Strong fragrances are discouraged — they
compete with the coffee and bother some guests.

## Food Safety and Allergens

Anyone handling food completes an allergen-awareness module in their first week.
Our kitchens handle milk, tree nuts, soy, wheat, and eggs; we cannot guarantee
any item is free of trace allergens. If a guest asks about an allergen, never
guess — pull up the allergen sheet in the app or ask a lead. Wash hands on every
shift change and after handling allergens. Milk pitchers for alternative milks are
color-coded and washed separately.

## Opening and Closing Procedures

Opening: disarm the alarm, start the espresso machine warm-up (20 minutes),
brew the first batch of drip, stock the pastry case from the morning delivery,
and count the till. Closing: brew nothing after the last call, break down and
clean the espresso group heads, wipe and sanitize all surfaces, take out compost
and recycling, count the till, and set the alarm. The closing checklist in the
app must be fully checked before you leave.

## Cash Handling

Tills start at $150 in counted change. If your drawer is more than $5 over or
under at cash-out, flag it to your lead and note it in the app — honest mistakes
happen and aren't held against you, but unreported ones are a problem. Large
bills go under the drawer. The safe is opened only by shift leads.

## Tips

Tips are pooled per café per day and split by hours worked. Tips are paid out
weekly, every Friday, through payroll — never in cash.

## Conduct and Respect

We have zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination of any kind, toward
teammates or guests. If something happens, tell any lead or use the anonymous
reporting form in the app; reports are taken seriously and retaliation is itself
a fireable offense. Treat guests with patience even when they test it, and treat
each other better than that.

## Performance Reviews

Reviews happen at 90 days and then every six months. They're a two-way
conversation, not a verdict — you'll also rate how we're doing as an employer.
Raises are tied to the training ladder and review outcomes.

## Leaving the Company

We ask for two weeks' notice. Return your apron and any café keys or fobs. Your
final paycheck includes any unused, accrued PTO up to the rollover cap. You're
always welcome back, and we're happy to be a reference.
