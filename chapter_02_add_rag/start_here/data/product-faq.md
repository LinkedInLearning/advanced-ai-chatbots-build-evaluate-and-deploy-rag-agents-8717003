# Roya's Imaginary Cafe — Product FAQ

## Where do your beans come from?

We buy directly from farms and co-ops in Colombia (Huila), Ethiopia
(Yirgacheffe), and Guatemala (Antigua). Every bag lists the farm, the elevation,
and the harvest date. If it doesn't say where it's from, we don't sell it. We
visit our partner farms at least once a year and publish what we pay above the
commodity price on our website.

## How is your decaf made?

Our decaf is processed using the Swiss Water method — no chemical solvents, just
water, temperature, and time. It removes 99.9% of the caffeine while keeping the
origin character of the bean. Our current decaf is a Colombian from the Huila
region. Decaf is available as espresso, drip, or by the bag, same price as our
regular coffee.

## How fresh is the coffee?

We roast in Cambridge every Monday and Thursday. Bags are stamped with the roast
date, and we never sell beans more than 21 days past roast. Anything older goes
to our cold brew program or staff bags. For peak flavor, we suggest using beans
within three weeks of the roast date and grinding right before you brew.

## What roast levels do you offer?

Three house profiles: a Light roast that leans bright and fruity (great for
pour-over), a Medium that's balanced and sweet (our most popular, and the base
for most espresso drinks), and a Dark that's bold and low-acid. Seasonal
single-origins rotate through as a fourth option. Tasting notes are printed on
every bag.

## What milk alternatives do you offer?

Oat, almond, and soy — all at no extra charge. Our oat milk is the barista
edition and steams almost identically to whole milk. We do not currently offer
coconut milk because it kept splitting in hot drinks. We also stock whole and
skim dairy, and a lactose-free dairy option on request.

## What is the Lantern?

The Lantern is our signature drink: a double shot of espresso, steamed milk, a
spoon of house-made honey-cinnamon syrup, and a dusting of cocoa. It was invented
by our first barista, Priya, during a power outage in 2018, and it's been on the
menu ever since. It's available hot or iced, and decaf on request.

## What else is on the espresso menu?

The usual lineup — espresso, macchiato, cortado, flat white, cappuccino, latte,
and mocha — plus the Lantern. Syrups are made in-house: vanilla, honey-cinnamon,
and a seasonal flavor. We pull a traditional 1:2 ratio shot by default and are
happy to pull it longer or shorter if you have a preference.

## Do you have seasonal drinks?

Yes. Spring brings a honey-lavender latte, summer a citrus cold foam cold brew,
autumn a maple-spice latte, and winter a peppermint mocha. Seasonal menus change
on the first of the relevant month and run while supplies last.

## What teas do you carry?

A small, rotating selection of loose-leaf teas from a Boston-area importer: an
English breakfast, a jasmine green, a rooibos, and a caffeine-free peppermint.
Teas are steeped to order. We also make a chai latte with a house-spiced
concentrate.

## Do you have food?

We carry pastries from a local bakery delivered fresh each morning —
croissants, a daily scone, banana bread, and a vegan oat bar. Pastries are not
made in our kitchens, so ask for the allergen sheet if you have a sensitivity.
Anything unsold at close goes to a community fridge, never the trash.

## What's in your cold brew?

Cold brew is steeped 18 hours from a coarse-ground medium roast and our
slightly-older-but-still-good beans. It's served black, over ice, or with cold
foam. We also make a nitro cold brew at the Cambridge and Somerville locations.

## Do you sell brewing equipment?

Yes — each café stocks a small selection of pour-over drippers, filters, scales,
and grinders. Employees get 30% off equipment. We will happily grind any bag you
buy to match your brewer, whether that's espresso, drip, pour-over, or French
press.

## How should I store my beans at home?

Keep them in an airtight container away from light, heat, and moisture — a
cupboard, not the fridge or freezer, which introduce condensation. Buy what
you'll drink in two to three weeks rather than stocking up. Whole bean keeps far
longer than pre-ground.

## Do you offer a coffee subscription?

Yes. Pick a bag size and a cadence (weekly, biweekly, or monthly) and we ship or
hold for pickup on roast day. Subscriptions can pause or change anytime in the
app, and subscribers get 10% off equipment.

## Is your packaging recyclable?

Our bags are made from a compostable kraft material, and the tin ties are
removable for recycling. Cold cups are still plastic — we're testing compostable
alternatives in the Cambridge café through the end of the year. Bring your own
clean cup and we'll knock 25 cents off the drink.
