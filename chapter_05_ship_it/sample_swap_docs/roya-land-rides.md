# Roya Land — Rides & Attractions

Welcome to Roya Land, the imaginary theme park where every ride is powered by a
little bit of magic. This guide covers our headline attractions, how thrilling
each one is, and who can ride.

## At a glance

- **The most exciting ride:** **Dragon's Ascent** — the only ride at Roya Land
  with a full 5-star thrill rating.
- **Tallest ride:** **Chronos Plunge**, a time-bending drop tower that falls 90 metres.
- **Best ride for little wizards:** **Starlight Carousel**, gentle enough for all
  ages with no height requirement.
- **Newest attraction (opened this season):** **Mistcaller's Flight**.

## The rides

### Dragon's Ascent · Thrill 5/5 · Emberhold
Our signature coaster and the most exciting ride in the park. You're strapped to
the back of an enchanted dragon that "takes flight," looping over the volcano at
Emberhold before a near-vertical dive. It holds a perfect 5-star thrill rating —
the highest at Roya Land — and it's the ride guests line up for first.
**Height requirement: 122 cm.**

### Chronos Plunge · Thrill 4/5 · Clockwork Quarter
The tallest ride at Roya Land at 90 metres. A magical drop tower that "rewinds
time" on the slow climb up, then lets go all at once.
**Height requirement: 132 cm** — the strictest in the park.

### Mistcaller's Flight · Thrill 4/5 · Cloudspire
A suspended flying-theater ride that soars over misty peaks while a mistcaller
conjures storms around you. Opened this season.
**Height requirement: 107 cm.**

### Goblin Market Dash · Thrill 3/5 · The Underwarren
An interactive dark ride through a bustling goblin bazaar — grab a wand and score
points by zapping mischievous goblins as your cart weaves through the stalls.
Fun for most ages. **Height requirement: 97 cm.**

### The Whispering Woods · Thrill 2/5 · Verdant Glade
A slow enchanted boat ride through a forest of talking trees that lean in to tell
you their secrets. Calm and family-friendly. **No height requirement.**

### Starlight Carousel · Thrill 1/5 · Celestial Court
A floating carousel of constellation creatures — ride a glowing lion or a swan
made of stars. Our gentlest attraction, open to all ages.
**No height requirement.**

## Thrill ratings, explained
We rate every ride from 1 star (gentle, all ages) to 5 stars (maximum intensity).
Only **Dragon's Ascent** earns a 5. If you want the biggest thrill in the park,
that's the one to head for.
