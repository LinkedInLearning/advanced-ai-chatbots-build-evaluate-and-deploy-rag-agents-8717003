# Roya Land — Guest Policies & Safety

## Height requirements
For safety, some rides have a minimum height:

- **Chronos Plunge** — 132 cm
- **Dragon's Ascent** — 122 cm
- **Mistcaller's Flight** — 107 cm
- **Goblin Market Dash** — 97 cm
- **The Whispering Woods** and **Starlight Carousel** — no minimum; open to all ages.

Guests who don't meet a ride's height requirement can enjoy our many all-ages
attractions instead.

## The Magical Weather Guarantee
Roya Land runs rain or shine. If a ride closes because of a storm for more than an
hour during your visit, your Day Pass automatically converts to a free return
ticket — no need to ask. We call it the **Magical Weather Guarantee**.

## Refunds
Day Passes and Twilight Passes are refundable up to 24 hours before your visit.
Season Passes are non-refundable, but transferable to another guest once per year.

## Accessibility
Every themed land is step-free, and the Skyline Tram is wheelchair accessible.
Guests with accessibility needs can request a **Gentle Access** wristband at the
main gate for step-free boarding on every ride.

## Lost & found
Lost something magical? Visit the Lantern Office near the main gate. Found items
are held for 30 days.
