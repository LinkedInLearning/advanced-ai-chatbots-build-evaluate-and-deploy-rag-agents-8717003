# Roya Land — Visitor Guide

Everything you need to plan a day at Roya Land.

## Park hours
Roya Land is open **daily, 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM**. On summer weekends
(June through August) the park stays open late until **11:00 PM** for our nightly
Starfall fireworks over the Celestial Court.

## Tickets
- **Day Pass** — full access to every ride, all day.
- **Twilight Pass** — discounted entry after 5:00 PM, valid until close.
- **Season Pass** — unlimited visits for a year, plus early entry 30 minutes
  before opening.

Children under 3 always enter free.

## Roya FastPass
Skip the queue with a **Roya FastPass** — an enchanted wristband that reserves
your ride time. Tap it at any ride entrance to hold your place in line while you
explore the park. Each FastPass includes reservations for up to four rides per
day; Dragon's Ascent and Chronos Plunge count as "premium" reservations and use
two of your four slots each.

## Dining
- **The Gilded Griffin** — sit-down feasts in Emberhold, right beside Dragon's Ascent.
- **Pixie Provisions** — grab-and-go snacks and enchanted lemonade, with carts
  throughout the park.
- **The Floating Feast** — a dessert cafe that hovers above the Celestial Court.

## Getting here
Roya Land has a large enchanted car park — follow the glowing lanterns to your row
so you never lose your car. The Skyline Tram runs from the main gate to every
themed land, arriving every few minutes.
